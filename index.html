Hi!

My name is Max Blackbourn, and I’m currently a third-year student at Harvard University studying “AI & Society”, a degree I designed myself.

I believe we are in an extremely critical window over the next few years to safeguard AI better, before we roll the dice on whether superintelligence will permanently disempower, and perhaps kill, us all.

There are billions of current lives, and perhaps trillions of future lives, all at significant risk from superintelligent AI.

I think the point of no return is rapidly approaching (roughly 6 months to 1.5 years away), so I’m desperately trying to use all of my time to contribute in the most effective capacity I can.
